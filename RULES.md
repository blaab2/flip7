# Flip 7 Rules

This is a summary in our own words of the publisher's rulebook, **Ruleset Edition 3.1**. If this summary and the rulebook disagree, the rulebook wins.

Official rulebook (PDF): <https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-asmodeeca/uploads/2025/12/FLIP_7-RULES.pdf>

Flip 7 is designed by Eric Olsen and published by The Op (USAopoly).

## Objective

Score the most points in a press-your-luck race to **200**. Each round, collect number cards and decide when to stop. A duplicate number loses you the whole round.

## The deck (94 cards)

| Type | Cards | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Number | One 0, one 1, and *n* copies of each value *n* from 2 to 12 (twelve 12s, eleven 11s, and so on) | 79 |
| Score modifier | One each of +2, +4, +6, +8, +10, x2 | 6 |
| Action | Three each of Freeze, Flip Three, Second Chance | 9 |

Higher numbers score more but are more likely to cause a bust, because there are more copies of them.

The game takes 3 or more players. The rulebook recommends a second deck for more than 18.

## Playing a round

1. **Deal.** The dealer gives each player, including themself, one card face up, in turn order. If an action card comes up, pause and resolve it immediately, then continue until everyone has been dealt a card. After the deal, a player may have no cards, or three or four.
2. **Hit or stay.** The dealer offers each active player in turn the choice:
   - **Hit:** take another card.
   - **Stay:** leave the round and bank your points. You may only stay if you have at least one card in front of you.
3. **Bust.** If you receive a number you already have, you are out of the round and score nothing.

An **active player** is one who has not busted or stayed. Inactive players turn their cards over until the round ends.

### The round ends when

- no active players remain, or
- a player has **7 unique number cards** (a "Flip 7"). This ends the round immediately for everyone, and the other players score whatever they have at that moment, unless they busted.

Only number cards count toward a Flip 7. The 0 counts as a number card even though it is worth no points.

## Action cards

An action card can be played on **any active player, including yourself**. If you are the only active player, you must play it on yourself.

- **Freeze:** the player receiving it banks their points and is out of the round.
- **Flip Three:** the player receiving it must take the next three cards, flipping them one at a time.
  - Number, modifier and action cards all count toward the three.
  - Stop early if the player busts or achieves a Flip 7.
  - A Second Chance revealed during the three may be set aside and used.
  - Any other Flip Three or Freeze revealed is resolved **after** all three cards are drawn, and only if the player has not busted.
- **Second Chance:** keep it in front of you. If you are given a number you already have, discard both the Second Chance and the duplicate, and you do not bust.
  - You may only have one at a time. If you are dealt another, give it to another active player.
  - If no other player is active, or all of them already have one, discard it.
  - All Second Chance cards are discarded at the end of the round, used or not.

## Modifier cards

Modifiers are not number cards. They can't make you bust and don't count toward a Flip 7. If you end a round with only modifiers, you still score them, but x2 alone scores nothing.

## Scoring a round

Score in this order:

1. Add up your number cards.
2. If you have the x2 card, double that sum. It doubles number cards only.
3. Add any +2 to +10 modifiers.
4. If you achieved a Flip 7, add 15.

A player who busted scores 0.

**Example:** numbers 3, 5, 7, 11 and 10 total 36. With x2 and +4, the score is 36 x 2 + 4 = **76**.

**Example:** numbers 3, 11, 5, 7, 10, 9 and 4 total 49. With the Flip 7 bonus, the score is 49 + 15 = **64**.

## Next round

Set all cards from the round aside. **Do not shuffle them back into the deck**, so earlier rounds' cards stay out of play and the rulebook advises keeping count of them. Pass the remaining deck to the left, and that player becomes the new dealer.

When the deck runs out, shuffle the discarded cards to form a new deck. If this happens mid-round, cards in front of players stay where they are, including those of busted players.

## End of the game

When at least one player has 200 or more points at the end of a round, the player with the most points wins.

## Not settled by the rulebook

Edition 3.1 doesn't explicitly answer these, so the organizers should decide them before play:

- [ ] How is a tie for the most points at the end of the game broken?
- [ ] Who takes the first hit-or-stay decision in a round?
- [ ] After using a Second Chance, does the player's turn end, or may they still choose to hit or stay?
- [ ] Which tools and devices may teams use during play? Are there time limits on decisions?
